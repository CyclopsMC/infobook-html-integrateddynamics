NBT Extractor Remote

The NBT Extractor Remote is an item-form companion to the NBT Extractor block. Right click any placed NBT Extractor to bind the remote to it. From that point on you can open the same NBT browsing interface from anywhere in the same dimension without having to stand next to the block. The remote requires line-of-sight loading: the target chunk must be loaded.
