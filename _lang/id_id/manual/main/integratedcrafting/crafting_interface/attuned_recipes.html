Attuned Recipes

Since the Attuned Crafting Interface exposes all recipes of its machine, it may hand the network more recipes than you want it to use: a Crafting Table alone accounts for hundreds. Its GUI therefore shows every recipe of the bound machine as a grid of output icons, where you decide which ones the network is allowed to craft.

Recipes with a green border are exposed to the network. Clicking one gives it a red border and greys it out, which removes it from the network, so that no new crafting job uses it. Clicking it again brings it back. Hovering over a recipe shows its output, its recipe name and the ingredients it needs.

To change many recipes at once, hold down your mouse button and drag over them. The recipe you start on decides whether the drag enables or disables, so one gesture does both.

The search field matches recipe names, mod names and recipe ids. The Enable, Disable and Invert buttons apply to whatever the search currently matches, which is what makes a machine with thousands of recipes workable: searching for minecraft: and pressing Disable hides every vanilla recipe in one go.
