Ender Storage

Storage Terminals can be ender-upgraded by right-clicking them with an Eye of Ender. Upgraded terminals emit ender particles, so you can tell them apart at a glance.

An ender-upgraded terminal gains an additional Ender Storage tab, which shows the contents of your personal Ender Chest. Just like with a regular Ender Chest, you can insert and extract items there, including by shift-clicking.

Because the Ender Chest is bound to you instead of to the network, this tab has no channel selector, search field or Variable filter slots.

Breaking an ender-upgraded Storage Terminal will give you back the Eye of Ender that was used to upgrade it.

Portable Storage Terminals can be ender-upgraded as well, by combining them with an Eye of Ender in a crafting grid. This retains the network the terminal was linked to.
